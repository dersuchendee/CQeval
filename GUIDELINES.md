# Annotation Guidelines

## Overview

For each Competency Question (CQ), annotate the following dimensions:

### source_deviation

Identify elements present in the CQ but not explicitly present in the source scenario.

### justified_in_scenario

For each element identified in `source_deviation`, determine whether it is justified by the scenario.

- **Yes** = the element can be reasonably inferred from the scenario.
- **No** = the element is not supported by the scenario.

### framing

Identify evaluative or non-neutral terms in the CQ.

Examples include terms such as:

- crucial
- fundamental
- ordinarily

### General Note

Annotate only when clearly present; avoid over-interpretation.

---

## Examples

### Example 1

#### Scenario

Anna wants to study how traditional dances have evolved over time and how they are performed in different regions.

#### CQ

> How ethnic traditional dances have evolved over the years?

#### Annotation

**source_deviation**

Yes (ethnic, years)

**justified_in_scenario**

- ethnic: No
- years: Yes (over time)

**framing**

Yes (ethnic)
